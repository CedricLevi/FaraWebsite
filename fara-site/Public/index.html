Stop a seizure, save a memory.

Fara is a passive implant for drug-resistant focal epilepsy. Placed during hippocampal transection, its thin conductive film is designed to block the electrical coupling that lets a seizure spread, with no battery, no leads and no programming.

Surgery can end seizures. Too often it costs memory.

Gold-standard surgical resection is an option for only half of patients with mesial temporal lobe epilepsy (MTLE), and leaves the other half gambling with their memory.

Fara is a passive implant to treat this drug-resistant focal epilepsy. Placed during hippocampal transection therapy, it's designed to block seizures from spreading without a battery, leads or programming, making a memory-saving procedure viable.

An electric wall

Seizures partly spread through ephaptic coupling, allowing them to jump over surgical cuts. Fara creates an electric wall that stops them in their tracks. Learn more

No power, no tuning

Fara works passively. There are no batteries or electrodes to replace, and no hospitals to return to. We strive to keep patients out of the EMU.

MRI & Biocompatible

Custom-built for the patient using titanium. Designed for long-term use without imaging complications.

Fits the existing procedure

Our implant supplements multiple hippocampal transection therapy. Fara is neurosurgeon-approved, receiving top marks in ease of adoption.
